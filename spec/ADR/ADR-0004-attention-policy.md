# ADR-0004 — Attention Is a Company Policy

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

The company controls explicit thresholds and escalation rules. Attention is distinct from information retention.

## Rationale

Aurum may know many things without interrupting humans. Relevance, materiality, urgency, and actionability determine surfacing.

## Consequence

The system can retain low-priority information while reserving human attention for configured or learned-important matters.
