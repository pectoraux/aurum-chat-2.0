# ADR-0007 — Organizational Capability Graph

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Capabilities are first-class and may be supplied by people, teams, agents, software, suppliers, subcontractors, or partners.

## Rationale

Training, hiring, automation, outsourcing, and agent recruitment are alternative ways to close capability gaps.

## Consequence

Workforce, software, agents, and external suppliers can be evaluated as alternative capability sources.
