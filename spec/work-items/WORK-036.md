# WORK-036 — Goals and Desired-State Model

## Objective

Implement explicit organizational goals and desired-state representations.

## Dependencies

WORK-004, WORK-007, WORK-008.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A goal has an objective, desired state/target, metric or threshold, horizon, owner, priority, evidence sources, and success criteria.
2. Goals are temporally versioned.
3. Goals are distinct from beliefs, observations, and attention policy.
4. Current world state can be compared to desired state.
5. Detected gaps retain evidence and uncertainty.
6. Goal changes are auditable.
7. Goals can influence attention and investigation without directly bypassing policy.
