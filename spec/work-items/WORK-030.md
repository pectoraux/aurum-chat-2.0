# WORK-030 — MD Control Surface

## Objective

Create the management interface for company policy, attention, goals, approvals, recommendations, workforce/agent oversight, notifications, and system explanations.

## Dependencies

WORK-022, WORK-027, WORK-028, WORK-036, WORK-037, WORK-038.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. MD can configure explicit policy.
2. MD can review organizational goals and desired-state gaps.
3. MD can review recommendations.
4. MD can approve/reject controlled actions.
5. Evidence and uncertainty are visible for material recommendations.
6. Agent lifecycle can be reviewed.
7. Learned policy proposals can be accepted/rejected explicitly.
8. Consequential decisions expose their audit trail.
9. Pending/acknowledged notifications and approval requests are visible.
10. Provider/model identity is shown as audit metadata where relevant, without becoming a business semantic dependency.
