# WORK-037 — Audit and Decision Evidence

## Objective

Implement the authoritative append-only audit capability for consequential cognition and actions.

## Dependencies

WORK-005, WORK-006, WORK-011, WORK-022, WORK-031.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Consequential observations/evidence can be referenced in audit records.
2. Belief/claim state used by a recommendation is reconstructable.
3. Policy evaluated and threshold crossings are recorded.
4. LLM/AI provider and model used for an execution are recorded.
5. Agent/runtime provider used for an execution is recorded.
6. Recommendations, approvals, executions, results, and outcomes are linked.
7. Audit records are append-only from the domain perspective.
8. Audit records are tenant-authorized and queryable.
9. Audit information cannot be used as a substitute for authoritative domain state.
10. A fixture proves reconstruction of a consequential decision from input through outcome.
