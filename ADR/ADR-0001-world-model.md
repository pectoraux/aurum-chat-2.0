# ADR-0001 — Company-in-Environment World Model

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Aurum's primary world model represents the company and its external environment in one temporal relational model.

## Rationale

Company wellbeing depends on interactions between internal state and external conditions. Separate "company knowledge" and "research" models would make impact reasoning unnecessarily disconnected.

## Consequence

External events can be related directly to company entities, capabilities, goals, risks, and processes.

## Rejected Alternative

A company-only knowledge base with separate research tooling.
