# WORK-020 — Workforce Intelligence

## Objective

Detect capability, workload, performance, and staffing patterns.

## Dependencies

WORK-019, WORK-013.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Signals are evidence-backed.
2. Alternative explanations can be represented.
3. Training, reassignment, hiring, automation, and support can be represented as alternatives.
4. Employment-impacting outputs are recommendations.
5. Material recommendations expose evidence and uncertainty.
